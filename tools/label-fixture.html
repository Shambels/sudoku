Label a fixture

Turns a real photo into a manifest entry for test-vision.html. Load the photo, type the clues, optionally click the four grid corners, then paste the entry into tools/fixtures/manifest.js. Corners are optional but worth the four clicks: they let grid detection be scored on its own rather than only through the final board.

Click top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left of the grid, in that order.
Arrow keys move, any digit fills and advances, 0 / space / backspace clears.

Paste it inside the window.SUDOKU_FIXTURES = [ ... ] array. Entries without the synthetic tag survive a regeneration of the synthetic set, so real photos are safe there.
